CREST STUDENT TIMETABLE LINK — INDEX.HTML PATCH

In your existing index.html, find the Student Workspace block in the left sidebar. Immediately after the Assignments & Grades button, insert:

<button class="folder-item-btn" onclick="window.location.href='timetable.html'" style="background:rgba(56,189,248,0.12);border-color:rgba(56,189,248,0.3);color:#fff;">
  <span><i class="fas fa-calendar-days left-icon" style="color:#38bdf8;"></i> Weekly Timetable</span>
  <i class="fas fa-arrow-right" style="color:#38bdf8;"></i>
</button>

Upload timetable.html into the same root folder as index.html and admin.html.
This patch is provided for reference; it is not automatically inserted into your existing index.html.
